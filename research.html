Research profile

Computation, mathematics, and applications

Dr. Taha's work connects numerical analysis and computer science with applications in nonlinear science, wave propagation, communications, and biology.

His research has been supported by agencies and institutions including NSF, DOE, DoD, USDA, and Northeastern University.
